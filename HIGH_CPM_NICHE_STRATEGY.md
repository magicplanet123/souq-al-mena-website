## QUALITY RULES - REMEMBER FOR ALL FUTURE POSTS

### ✅ ALWAYS DO:

1. **3-Pass Quality Check:**
   - Pass 1: Content check (keywords, links, clarity)
   - Pass 2: Format check (headers, images, paragraphs)
   - Pass 3: Security check (no business secrets, affiliate disclosures)

2. **Image Quality:**
   - Professional, eye-catching design
   - SEO-friendly alt text (100-125 characters)
   - Descriptive filenames (lowercase, hyphens)
   - 3-4 images per blog minimum
   - High contrast, readable

3. **SEO Optimization:**
   - Keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
   - Meta description (160 characters)
   - Internal links (3-5 to your services)
   - Schema markup (Article type)
   - Mobile responsive

4. **Affiliate Disclosure:**
   - Add: "This post contains affiliate links. We earn commission if you purchase."
   - Transparent, clear placement
   - Required by FTC/GDPR

5. **Security/Privacy:**
   - No business secrets (suppliers, pricing, operations)
   - No customer data
   - Educational focus only
   - Affiliate links disclosed

### ❌ NEVER DO:

- Random placeholder videos
- Duplicate content from previous blogs
- Keywords from low-CPM niches on high-CPM days
- Skip quality review
- Expose business operations
- Undisclosed affiliate links
- Poor quality images
- Overlapping headers (fixed now)
